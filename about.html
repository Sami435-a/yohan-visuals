About

Behind the camera.

A photographer working across three very different rooms — a bakery kitchen, the streets of Addis Ababa, and a church stage.

The story
More than a camera.
It's a way of seeing.

Yohan Visuals moves between three worlds. At the bakery, it's watching for the moment a glaze catches the light or a tart gets its final garnish, then shooting it before anyone touches the tray.

Around Addis Ababa, it's slowing down enough to notice how a building's facade holds the evening light, or how a street looks different at a certain hour — the city photographed the way locals actually experience it.

And at Christ Mission Church, it's capturing services — from Friday worship to events like Arise & Shine — as they happen, without staging or interrupting the moment.

— Yohan Visuals
